FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Answers to the most common questions about FITAI food scanning, accuracy, and data security.

How does FITAI's AI camera food scanner work?
When you snap or upload a meal photo, FITAI first runs an automated non-food safety gate. Once verified as food, our vision pipeline estimates ingredients, portion sizes, and volumes, mapping them against verified USDA FoodData Central nutritional values to calculate calories, protein, carbs, and fats in seconds.
What happens if I upload a picture of a non-food item?
FITAI includes an automated image classifier before processing. If an image contains furniture, electronics, documents, or other non-food subjects, the app immediately informs you on screen without attempting a false calorie breakdown, saving processing time and keeping your diary accurate.
Can I customize my daily calories and macronutrient goals?
Yes! In addition to automatic metabolic recommendations computed from your height, weight, sex, and activity level, you can edit your exact target calories, protein, carbs, fats, and water intake directly from your Profile settings anytime.
What happens to the food photos I upload?
Your photos are encrypted in transit and securely hosted in your private, authenticated account so you can view your meal history. We never sell your photos or health logs to third parties or data brokers.
How do I permanently delete my account and data?
You can delete your account at any time in the mobile app under Profile → Delete Account. Following a 2-step confirmation, all your profile details, biometric metrics, meal logs, weight entries, and CDN-hosted photos are permanently wiped from our databases.
Is FITAI intended for medical diagnosis or clinical treatment?
No. FITAI is an informational wellness and fitness tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or prevent clinical conditions (such as diabetes, renal disease, or eating disorders). Always consult a licensed healthcare professional before starting any calorie-deficit regimen. Caloric deficit goals are strictly prohibited for minors under 18.
