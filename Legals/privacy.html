01. Overview & Architectural Guarantees

This Privacy Policy explains how [FITAI, Inc. / Company Name] ("FITAI", "we", "us", or "our") collects, protects, uses, and discloses information when you use the FITAI mobile application (for iOS and Android) and related services (collectively, the "Service").

Core Privacy Principles Built Into Our Codebase
• Zero Advertising Data Monetization: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal biometric metrics, dietary records, or photographs with third-party advertising brokers or behavioral marketing networks.
• Strict Isolation by User ID: All database queries enforce PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS) policies requiring authentication as the verified account owner.
• Edge Image Downscaling: Meal images are optimized and downscaled before transmission to reduce digital footprint and network exposure.
• Permanent Data Purge: When you delete your account, an automated pipeline irreversibly wipes your database records and purges CDN-hosted media files.

02. Personal & Biometric Data We Collect

FITAI collects only the specific data fields required to calculate energy balance, track nutritional intake, and deliver meal recognition:

A. Account & Authentication Information

  • Email Address & User ID: Managed securely via Supabase Auth (auth.users) to authenticate sessions and isolate private data partitions.
  • Display Name: An optional user-specified nickname stored in profiles.display_name (maximum 80 characters).

B. Physical Characteristics & Biometric Baselines

To calibrate resting metabolic rate (BMR) and total daily energy expenditure (TDEE) via the Mifflin-St Jeor formula, the application collects:

  • Biological Sex for Calculation: Stored in user_nutrition_profiles.sex_for_calculation (male, female, or unspecified).
  • Date of Birth & Age Band: Stored in user_nutrition_profiles.birth_date and profiles.age_band to calibrate age-dependent metabolic decline and enforce minor safety protections.
  • Height: Height in centimeters (software-validated range: 100 cm to 260 cm).
  • Current Weight: Weight in kilograms (software-validated range: 30 kg to 300 kg).
  • Target Weight & Weekly Pace: Goal weight in kilograms and target adjustment rate (0.25 kg to 1.0 kg/week).
  • Activity Level & Workouts: Physical activity tier (sedentary, light, moderate, heavy, very_heavy) and weekly exercise frequency.

C. Dietary, Meal & Health Habit Logs

  • Meal Photographs: Food images captured using the device camera or selected from your photo library.
  • Meal Entries: Timestamped culinary records, food item descriptions, serving sizes, and calculated macronutrient values (calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber).
  • Hydration Check-ins: Daily water consumption logs stored in milliliters in hydration_logs.
  • Weight Check-in History: Historical body weight log entries stored in weight_entries.
  • Custom Nutrition Goals: User-configured target calories, protein, carb, fat, and hydration thresholds stored in user_nutrition_profiles.

D. Device Permissions

  • Camera Permission: Requested at runtime (android.permission.CAMERA) solely when you activate the meal scanner to capture food photos.
  • Photo Library Permission: Requested at runtime solely if you choose to select a meal photograph from your device gallery.
  • Local Device Storage: The application uses AsyncStorage to store client-side theme preferences (fitai_theme_mode) and authenticated session tokens.

03. How Your Information Is Used

We process your data strictly for legitimate application functionality:

  • Deterministic Energy Calculations: Calculating your Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE) in our dedicated open calculation engine (@fitai/nutrition-engine).
  • Computer Vision Food Analysis: Processing food imagery to detect culinary ingredients, estimate portion weights, and return macronutrient estimates.
  • Historical Progress & Habit Visualizations: Rendering daily calorie rings, macro balance progress, and weight trend lines across the mobile dashboard.
  • Safety Floor Enforcement: Evaluating incoming data against software-enforced caloric minimums (1,200 kcal for females, 1,500 kcal for males, 1,350 kcal unspecified) and preventing minor weight-loss regimens.

04. AI Meal Scanning & Computer Vision Pipeline

FITAI's meal scanning feature incorporates a specialized multi-stage privacy-conscious architecture:

  1. Local Pre-Flight Food Filter: Before photographs are processed by cloud vision models, an automated filter (food-classifier-service.ts) inspects the photograph. Non-food images (such as furniture, documents, or objects) are rejected on-device, preventing unnecessary data transmission.
  2. Real-Time Resolution Optimization: Prior to visual intelligence analysis, images are downscaled via ImageKit.io URL transformation (tr:w-1024,h-1024,c-at_max,q-85). This reduces bandwidth and strips extraneous image metadata.
  3. Isolated Vision Inference: The downscaled photograph is transmitted via a secure serverless worker (meal-scan-processor.ts on Trigger.dev) to the Google Gemini Vision API (gemini-3.5-flash-lite / gemini-3.8-flash). No user identifiers, email addresses, names, or biometric statistics are transmitted to Google Gemini — only the food image buffer and a standardized nutritional analysis prompt.
  4. User Review & Logging: The AI model returns estimated food items and grams. The user reviews and verifies the estimation before saving it to the database.

05. Third-Party Subprocessors & Cloud Partners

We partner with specialized cloud infrastructure providers to run our backend services. Each subprocessor is bound by contractual data protection agreements:

  • Supabase, Inc. (Cloud Database & Authentication): Hosts our managed PostgreSQL database and handles authentication sessions. Cloud servers are hosted in secure data centers with encrypted storage volumes.
  • ImageKit.io (Media Hosting & Real-Time Image CDN): Hosts uploaded meal images and performs real-time edge downscaling. Images are identified by random unique file IDs (image_kit_file_id).
  • Trigger.dev (Serverless Background Orchestration): Executes asynchronous meal scan processing jobs between Supabase, ImageKit, and Google AI.
  • Google Cloud / Gemini API (Computer Vision AI): Processes downscaled food images to perform culinary ingredient and macro recognition under Google Cloud's Generative AI terms.
  • USDA FoodData Central: Public domain database used by the app to resolve verified nutrient reference facts for raw foods. No personal user data is sent to the USDA.

06. Tracking, Telemetry & Advertising Posture

No Third-Party Advertising Trackers
FITAI contains zero third-party behavioral advertising SDKs. We do not integrate Facebook Pixel, Google AdMob, AppsFlyer, Adjust, or data broker telemetry SDKs in the mobile application.

We do not cross-track users across external websites or third-party applications. Cloud worker logs (via Trigger.dev and Supabase server logs) capture technical runtime metrics (e.g. execution duration, HTTP status codes, and scan failures) solely to diagnose software errors and maintain service availability.

07. Data Storage, Encryption & Security Controls

We implement strict technical and organizational measures to protect personal data:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application, Supabase, ImageKit, Trigger.dev, and the Google Gemini API occur exclusively over encrypted Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud databases and media assets are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS): Every user data table (profiles, user_nutrition_profiles, meals, meal_items, weight_entries, hydration_logs, recipes) enforces database-level RLS policies. Authenticated API tokens can only read and write rows where user_id = auth.uid().
  • Least-Privilege Secret Management: Database service keys, ImageKit private API keys, and Gemini API keys are isolated on backend serverless workers and never embedded in the client application bundle.

08. User Rights, Corrections & Permanent Account Deletion

You maintain complete control over your health information in the application:

A. Direct Self-Service Correction

You can edit your display name, weight, height, birth date, biological sex, primary goal, activity level, and custom calorie/macro targets directly inside the mobile app anytime via Profile → Personal Details or Profile → Nutrition Goals.

B. Irreversible Account & Media Deletion Pipeline

You may permanently delete your account and all associated records at any time directly in the mobile application via Profile → Delete Account.

Automated Cascade Deletion Process
Upon two-step confirmation, the application executes an automated purge pipeline:
  • CDN Photo Purge: Authenticated REST API calls are sent to ImageKit (DELETE https://upload.imagekit.io/api/v1/files/${fileId}) permanently purging every meal scan image uploaded by your account.
  • Database Cascade Wipe: All corresponding rows across meal_scans, meals, weight_entries, hydration_logs, user_nutrition_profiles, and profiles are permanently deleted from Supabase.
  • Client Session Reset: Authenticated auth tokens and local device caches in AsyncStorage are immediately cleared.

C. Data Access & Portability Requests

Because automated PDF/CSV export is not currently offered as a self-service feature in the mobile client, you may request a copy of your personal dietary records and biometric history by emailing support@fitai.app. We will fulfill verified requests within thirty (30) days without charge.

09. Minor Protection & Pediatric Guardrails

FITAI is committed to the protection and nutritional safety of minors:

Software-Enforced Pediatric Restrictions
Under our database schema (guard_minor_weight_loss_goal and complete_onboarding RPC), any account indicating an age under 18 is strictly prohibited from selecting caloric deficits or weight-loss plans. Minor accounts are restricted to non-deficit baseline habits to prevent pediatric nutritional deprivation.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without verifiable parental consent. If we discover that an account was created by a child under 13 without appropriate guardian consent, we will immediately delete the account and purge all stored data.
